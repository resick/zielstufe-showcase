# Zielstufe

Zielstufe has mock German exams for nurses and doctors who need a German certificate to get their professional recognition in Germany.
Live site: https://zielstufe.com (English and German)

## What it does
- Mock exams in the official format and timing for telc Deutsch B1·B2 Pflege, Goethe-Test PRO Pflege, telc/Goethe/ÖSD B2, TestDaF and more
- For doctors: Fachsprachprüfung practice with an AI patient, plus the handover email, doctor's letter and case presentation
- Every answer comes with an explanation, and each section gets a score and an estimated level
- AI marks written answers against the exam criteria and gives specific corrections (Claude API)
- Works on phone or computer, and answers save as you go
- Clinics, care homes and agencies can buy seats for their learners and see their progress if the learner agrees
