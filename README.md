# Zielstufe

Mock German exams for nurses and doctors who need a German certificate
for their professional recognition in Germany.
Live site: https://zielstufe.com (English and German)

## What it does
- Mock exams in the official format and timing for telc Deutsch B1·B2 Pflege,
  Goethe-Test PRO Pflege, telc/Goethe/ÖSD B2, TestDaF and more
- For doctors: Fachsprachprüfung practice with an AI patient, handover email,
  doctor's letter and case presentation
- Every answer is explained, and each section gets a score and an estimated level
- Written answers are scored by AI against the exam criteria, with concrete
  corrections (Claude API)
- Works on phone or computer; answers are saved as you go
- Employers (clinics, care homes, agencies) can buy seats for their learners
  and see their progress, with the learner's consent
